The process
    Frequently asked questions
    The doubts investors raise most often
    1Can I have more than 10 foreign employees?›
    Venezuelan labor law asks that at least 90% of your staff be Venezuelan (the 90/10 rule), so foreign hires are generally capped at 10% of payroll. For specialized roles you can request an exception from the Ministry of Labor — we prepare the filing and the work permits for you.
    2Do I need to be in Venezuela to do all this?›
    Most steps can start remotely if you grant us a power of attorney. Only a few — opening the bank account and your biometric ID — need one in-person visit, which we schedule together so you resolve everything in a single trip.
    3Can I buy real estate as a foreigner?›
    Yes. Once you have your tax ID (RIF) and residence card you can buy property in your name or your company's. We review the title and handle the notary and the registry.
    4How do I repatriate profits?›
    Dividends are converted and transferred abroad through the central bank's (BCV) foreign-exchange framework. We keep your accounting and tax filings in order so the transfer clears without friction.
    5How much capital do I need to open a company?›
    There's no fixed legal minimum for a C.A.; you declare capital in line with your business plan. We help you size it so it's credible for banking and permits without over-committing.
    6How long does it take to set up a company?›
    About 2–3 weeks from the moment you sign the deed. The company's tax ID (RIF) follows automatically, and banking usually adds 1–2 weeks.
    7What taxes will my company pay?›
    Mainly corporate income tax (ISLR), 16% VAT (IVA) and a municipal business tax, plus payroll contributions (IVSS, INCES, FAOV). We register you with each and keep your clearances current.
